<intro>
A preview into my work for LuminAi, a pedagogical software for dancers to improvise with an AI dance agent. I am responsible for the 3D avatar design (the base mesh is from CC4) and an interactive shader that visualizes arcs of movement among other features. I also created an FX control panel to control LuminAi's appearance for live performance. The newest version of LuminAi premiered in an NSF-funded dance performance at Kennesaw State University in May 2024! See more from the Expressive Machinery Lab [here](https://expressivemachinery.gatech.edu/).
</intro>
